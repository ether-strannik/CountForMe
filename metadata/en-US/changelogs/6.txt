Themes carry their sounds. A theme is colours, the sound of every
cue and the spoken counts, in one folder. Nord ships; make your own
from blank or from a copy under Settings, Themes, edit colours and
sounds in place, and see it live. Export a theme as a zip, import
one from anywhere.

A preset category can name a theme. Picking a preset from it puts
that theme on.

A countdown timer rings with any sound in the theme's library.
