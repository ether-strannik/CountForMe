Music, and the cues cut through it. Play your own music in the app
and every cue ducks the song: it drops as the cue lands and comes
back between, by however much and however fast you set under
Settings, Music.

A theme carries its music as well as its sounds. A theme is now a
folder of two: sounds for the cues and the spoken counts, media for
the songs. A zip carries the whole of it.

A player behind the strip above the tabs: the queue with each song's
length, seek, previous and next, shuffle and repeat. A folder browser
beside it walks the music folders you add under Settings, and playing
a song makes that folder the queue.

Music keeps playing off screen and on the lock screen, with the song
named there and the transport on it. The app comes back to what it
was playing, at the place it was left.

A preset category says whether it takes its theme's music. Importing
presets asks how much of a named theme to apply.

still counting down.
