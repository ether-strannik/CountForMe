Music, and the cues cut through it. Play your own and every cue ducks
the song: it drops as the cue lands and comes back between, by however
much and however fast you set.

A theme carries music as well as sounds, and a zip carries the whole
of it. A player behind the strip: the queue, seek, shuffle, repeat,
and a browser for your own folders.

It plays off screen and on the lock screen, and comes back where it
was left.
