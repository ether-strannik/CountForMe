A Volume tab in settings: a fader for the cues, one for the spoken
counts, each with a Test button. The counts are louder to begin with,
so they sit level with the cues.

Sounds gains a Timer row, setting what a new timer starts with.

Cadence refuses a range that is not a whole number of its cadence. A
5:00 range at a cue every 45s used to lose one silently. The setup
line counts cycles now, like the run screen.

Phases keeps the last cycle of a block instead of dropping it.
