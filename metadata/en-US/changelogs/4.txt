A Volume tab in settings: one fader for the cues, one for the spoken
counts, each with a Test button. The spoken counts are louder to
begin with, so they sit level with the cues.

Sounds gains a Timer row, setting what a new countdown timer starts
with.

Cadence refuses a range that is not a whole number of its cadence. A
range of 5:00 at a cue every 45 seconds used to lose one silently.
The setup line also counts cycles now, like the run screen.

Phases keeps the last cycle of a block instead of dropping it.
